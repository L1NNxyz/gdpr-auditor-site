What the application is

The application is a tool that runs on your own computer. It examines your own email, with your permission, to identify services you hold accounts with, and helps you compose data protection requests to those services.

It is provided for your personal use in exercising your own rights over your own data. You must not use it to access anyone else's mailbox without their informed consent.

No legal advice

The request templates are informational starting points. They are not legal advice, and using them creates no professional relationship of any kind.

Data protection law varies by jurisdiction and changes over time. Whether a particular company must respond to you, within what period, and subject to what exemptions, depends on circumstances this application cannot assess. If the outcome matters, consult a qualified adviser or your national data protection authority.

Accuracy

The application identifies accounts by interpreting patterns in email. It is neither exhaustive nor always correct. It will miss accounts whose mail you have deleted or that never sent identifiable mail, and it will sometimes list a company you do not hold an account with. Confidence scores indicate relative certainty; they are not guarantees.

Check results before acting on them.
