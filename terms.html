7. Quality and defects

It will miss accounts whose mail you have deleted or that never sent identifiable mail, and it will sometimes list a company you do not hold an account with. Confidence scores express a relative assessment; they are not assurances.

Check results before acting on them. In particular, verify a discovered contact address before using it: addresses marked "unchecked" were read automatically from a page and have not been confirmed.
